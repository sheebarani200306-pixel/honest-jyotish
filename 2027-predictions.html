About the 2027 Predictions

The year 2027 brings important planetary movements according to the traditional Vedic sidereal zodiac. Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu are considered important influences in yearly horoscope interpretation.

These predictions are written according to Janma Rashi, meaning the Moon sign at birth. They are general interpretations and are not personalised birth-chart readings.

For a personalised prediction, the Ascendant, Moon degree, Nakshatra, planetary strengths, divisional charts and Vimshottari Dasha must also be considered.

Major Planetary Transits of 2027

Jupiter - Guru

  • Begins the year in Leo.
  • Returns to Cancer around 25 January.
  • Enters Leo around 26 June.
  • Enters Virgo around 26 November.

Saturn - Shani

  • Begins the year in Pisces.
  • Enters Aries around 3 June.
  • Returns to Pisces around 20 October.

Rahu and Ketu

  • Rahu remains in Capricorn under the Lahiri calculation used here.
  • Ketu remains in Cancer.

Transit dates are approximate Indian calendar dates. Mean-node and true-node calculations can differ.
