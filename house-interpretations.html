✦ The 12 Houses of Vedic Astrology ✦

Learn what each house traditionally represents in a Vedic birth chart.

What are the 12 Houses?

In traditional Vedic astrology, a birth chart is divided into 12 houses. Each house represents a different area of life. The signs and planets placed in these houses are interpreted together.

House 1 · Lagna Bhava

1st House – Self and Personality

Represents: Physical appearance, body, identity, personality, confidence and general approach to life.

Traditional theme: Self, beginnings and personal identity.

House 2 · Dhana Bhava

2nd House – Wealth and Family

Represents: Savings, family, speech, food, possessions and personal values.

Traditional theme: Resources, family roots and communication.

House 3 · Sahaja Bhava

3rd House – Courage and Communication

Represents: Courage, siblings, communication, writing, skills, hobbies and short journeys.

Traditional theme: Effort, initiative and self-expression.

House 4 · Sukha Bhava

4th House – Home and Happiness

Represents: Mother, home, property, vehicles, education, emotional comfort and inner peace.

Traditional theme: Roots, security and belonging.

House 5 · Putra Bhava

5th House – Creativity and Learning

Represents: Education, intelligence, creativity, children, romance, hobbies and traditional mantra practice.

Traditional theme: Creativity, knowledge and expression.

House 6 · Ari Bhava

6th House – Challenges and Service

Represents: Daily work, service, discipline, debts, competition, obstacles and traditional health indications.

Traditional theme: Problem-solving, responsibility and perseverance.

House 7 · Yuvati Bhava

7th House – Relationships and Partnership

Represents: Marriage, partnerships, business relationships, agreements and public interactions.

Traditional theme: Cooperation, commitment and relationships.

House 8 · Randhra Bhava

8th House – Transformation and Mystery

Represents: Sudden changes, inheritance, shared resources, research, secrets and transformation.

Traditional theme: Change, depth and hidden matters.

House 9 · Dharma Bhava

9th House – Wisdom and Philosophy

Represents: Higher education, teachers, father, philosophy, spirituality, ethics and long-distance journeys.

Traditional theme: Wisdom, beliefs and higher learning.

House 10 · Karma Bhava

10th House – Career and Actions

Represents: Career, profession, reputation, responsibilities, achievements and public standing.

Traditional theme: Work, contribution and responsibility.

House 11 · Labha Bhava

11th House – Gains and Networks

Represents: Income, gains, ambitions, friendships, social networks and fulfilment of goals.

Traditional theme: Community, aspirations and gains.

House 12 · Vyaya Bhava

12th House – Spirituality and Release

Represents: Expenses, sleep, solitude, foreign places, retreats, charity and spiritual reflection.

Traditional theme: Letting go, reflection and liberation.

Honest Jyotish Disclaimer

These descriptions explain traditional Vedic astrology beliefs. Astrology is not scientifically established as a method for predicting personality or future events. A house interpretation alone cannot determine a person's life.
